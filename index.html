04 / About section

Keep the principles. Sharpen the story.

Your existing About section has a strong human voice and specific proof. The rewrite below makes the audience, practical help and business context easier to scan.

What works now

The emphasis on clear communication, doing the work properly and treating decisions seriously feels authentic. Specific business descriptions and credentials add substance.

What to improve

The section moves quickly between several businesses and roles. A clearer opening and shorter, separated paragraphs can help readers understand the through-line before they reach the details.

Your proposed About
Property decisions deserve clear advice, careful management and people who take responsibility. My work sits across property, landlord support and finance. Across those areas, I come back to the same principles: do the work properly, communicate clearly and treat every decision like it matters. At RGA Property Solutions, we support landlords and property owners with residential sales, lettings, property management and compliance. We believe property should be looked after with the same care and attention we would expect for our own. The team is trusted by 400+ landlords, and our focus is on personal service, clear communication and practical support. My wider experience includes mortgage and protection broking, portfolio thinking and property-business expansion. At Pack Holidays, I contributed to portfolio expansion, including work to grow a collection of managed holiday homes from 25 to more than 60, with a focus on distinctive, dog-friendly and accessible properties. I’m interested in the details that make property ownership work better: how an asset is managed, how risks and compliance are handled, how decisions are explained, and how owners can take a more informed view of their portfolios. My qualifications and memberships include MARLA, MNAEA, CeMAP and DEA, alongside postgraduate study in Real Estate Investment & Finance. If you’re a landlord, property owner or operator navigating a property decision, I value straightforward conversations grounded in practical experience.
